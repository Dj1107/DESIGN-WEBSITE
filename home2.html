Protect Yourself from Online Fraud

E-commerce fraud is a growing concern. By 2030, the global B2C e-commerce market is projected to be worth around $7.9 trillion. Malicious actors are keen to exploit this by scamming online buyers. Learn how to identify and avoid scams to ensure a safe online shopping experience.

Tips to Stay Safe While Shopping Online

  • 🔐 Use a secure payment system.
  • 👤 Verify website's contact information.
  • ✅ Ensure you receive a confirmation email.
  • 💸 Check for a clear return/refund policy.
  • 🔒 Use a secure internet connection.
  • 📚 Research the website's reputation.
  • 🚩 Be aware of red flags for fake websites.
  • 🔔 Pay attention to the website's URL.
  • 💻 Keep your software and antivirus up-to-date.
  • 📧 Don't click on suspicious links in emails or messages.

E-commerce Fraud Solutions

Here are some solutions that e-commerce businesses can implement to mitigate fraud:

  • 🔑 Implement multi-factor authentication (MFA).
  • 👮 Use Address Verification System (AVS) and Card Verification Value (CVV) checks.
  • 💻 Employ AI-powered fraud detection systems.
  • 💳 Use reputable payment gateways.
  • 📈 Conduct regular security assessments.
  • 👤 Provide information to customers about scams.
  • 🕵 Vet affiliates and validate purchases.
  • 💾 Use attribution tracking tools.

Report an E-commerce Scam

If you've been a victim of an e-commerce scam, it's important to report it. This helps authorities track and combat fraud. You can report it to the authorities, a consumer protection agency, or the e-commerce platform where the scam occurred.
