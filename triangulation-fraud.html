Triangulation Fraud

Triangulation fraud is a sophisticated type of e-commerce scam that involves three parties: the scammer, the unsuspecting buyer, and a legitimate online retailer. The scammer sets up a fake online storefront to collect payments and customer information but never intends to ship the advertised goods themselves.

How Triangulation Fraud Works

The process typically unfolds in the following stages:

  • Fake Online Storefront: The scammer creates a seemingly legitimate online store, often offering popular items at significantly discounted prices to attract buyers.
  • Buyer Places Order: An unsuspecting buyer finds the attractive offer and places an order on the fake storefront, providing their payment details (credit card information) and shipping address.
  • Scammer Purchases from Legitimate Retailer: Using the stolen credit card information (often acquired through other means or sometimes even the buyer's own details), the scammer purchases the same or a similar item from a legitimate online retailer.
  • Direct Shipment to Buyer: The scammer provides the buyer's shipping address to the legitimate retailer. The retailer ships the item directly to the buyer, who believes they have received their order from the initial fake storefront.
